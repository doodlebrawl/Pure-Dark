Tips

  1. When the walls turn blue and the screen darkens, Pure Dark is close. Don’t freeze. Turn away and keep moving.
  2. Open the Bag and equip the tracker in slot 1. It shows how far Pure Dark is and which way. Check it at every junction.
  3. Glowing rooms and oversized boxes are hiding spots. Duck in early, but hiding costs stamina, so slip out once the warning fades.
  4. A racing heartbeat means danger is near. Wear headphones: you’ll hear trouble before you see it.
  5. Sprinting is loud and tiring. Sprint in short bursts, then walk to recover. Running also lets the hunter notice you from farther away.
  6. Your lantern burns down. Grab every piece of tinder to relight it. Fill bottles at streams or broken pipes, then drink to recover stamina faster.
  7. Bellkeepers sell gear for timber and will hide you behind their desk, but only for about 14 seconds. The Usher asks them where you went.
  8. Save the Flare (stuns entities for 7s) for emergencies. The Doll lures hostiles away, though not the Usher.
  9. The tall figure at the end of a corridor can’t hurt you. Don’t stare. Look away and take another route.
  10. Fall down a pothole and you’ll reach the Silicon Hallways. Keep your light low there. Controls: left thumb or WASD to move, drag the right side to look, E to hide.
